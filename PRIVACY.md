# Tafind privacy policy

Publisher: **Talgat Ashim**. Effective date: 3 November 2026.

## Scope

This policy describes Tafind, a skills-only public-source social research workflow for ChatGPT and Codex, and this GitHub information/support repository.

## Research data

Tafind has no publisher-operated server, database, telemetry or analytics service. It does not automatically send prompts, research results, exports or local processing databases to Talgat Ashim.

Research is carried out by tools available in your host. Queries and visited pages are processed by the host, search providers and source websites as needed for your request. Their terms, privacy policies and retention settings apply. This policy does not change how those services handle data.

Results remain in your chat and, when you request supported local processing or exports, in files or databases in your environment. You control these local artifacts. Remove them through your filesystem and manage chat retention/deletion through your host. Deleting an export does not automatically delete the source chat or other copies.

## Support

Support uses public GitHub Issues. Information you choose to post is public and may be read by the publisher and others. Do not post personal, confidential or credential data. GitHub operates the repository and applies its own privacy and retention policies. The publisher does not set a separate automatic deletion period for GitHub issues and cannot promise removal of third-party copies.

## Your choices and contact

You can stop using Tafind, uninstall it, manage your host data settings and remove local files. To ask a privacy question or request removal of content under the publisher's control, open a GitHub issue without including sensitive details: https://github.com/ashimov-commits/tafind/issues . Requests are reviewed within the controls available on GitHub; deletion from third-party services must be requested from those services.

Material changes to this policy will be recorded in this repository with an updated effective date.
