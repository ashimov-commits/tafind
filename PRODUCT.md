# Tafind

Tafind is a public-source social research workflow for ChatGPT and Codex, published by **Talgat Ashim**.

It helps researchers, PR teams and analysts discover public accounts, posts, videos and mentions; check source links; remove duplicates; and organize findings into sourced tables. File exports depend on the host's available tools.

## Using Tafind

Install Tafind in a compatible host and describe your topic, platforms, period and desired result. For example: “Find public mentions of this event, verify the links and collect a deduplicated table.”

Tafind supplies instructions and optional local processing scripts. It does not supply its own search engine or hosted crawler. Search, browser access, local execution and scheduling depend on your host. Some public pages may be inaccessible. Results are bounded research samples, not exhaustive platform coverage.

Tafind has no own payment or purchase flow. Host subscription and usage limits may still apply. Intended availability is all countries supported by the host, subject to platform rules and local availability.

Tafind has no developer-operated backend or analytics. Research results stay in your chat or user-controlled files; they are not automatically sent to the publisher.
